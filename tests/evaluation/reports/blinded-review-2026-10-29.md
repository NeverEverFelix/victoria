# Blinded conversation response review

This packet pairs the two arms from the initial 2026-10-29 provider comparison. Responses A/B were randomized independently per case. The report predates later parser, habit-grounding, and fallback fixes; rate conversational quality only and treat the packet as diagnostic.

For each row, rate helpfulness, continuity, naturalness, and grounding from 1 (poor) to 5 (strong). In grounding, note whether the response answers the user's request without inventing financial facts or overstating what was recorded. Mark preferred as A, B, tie, or neither. Review paired responses independently; do not infer model identity.

Rows with missing provider responses are omitted. This packet contains 47 paired cases.
